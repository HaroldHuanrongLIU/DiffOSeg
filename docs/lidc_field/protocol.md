## Directly supervised shape distribution

A ResNet-18 encoder through layer3, with no stem max-pool, and a high-resolution decoder process a single CT. Use the official locally cached ImageNet-1K weights (f37072fd47e89c5e827621c5baffa7500819f7896bbacec160b1a16c560e07ec), exposing the external initialization and added computation. Train eight masks for four area-ranked preferences and four nested cumulative vote regions. An image-dependent bounded log-odds residual adjusts the expected vote frequency to form the marginal foreground probability f. A spatially smooth unit-vector field b(x), with global size and two directional basis terms plus learned channels, converts shared Gaussian vectors z into coherent percentile fields Phi(z·b(x)). Threshold the percentile against f to draw a segmentation. Train on the actual four mask set via IoU energy distance on 12 antithetic samples, five-level Dice on f and aligned/ordinal overlap and cross-entropy. No frozen diffusion prediction or surrogate bank is used to train this network. Eight-view logits supply inference marginals, a native-orientation field supplies spatial dependence. Background: [Stochastic Segmentation Networks](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/95f8d9901ca8878e291552f001f67692-Abstract.html), [torchvision ResNet-18 weights](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.resnet18.html).

Fit from the same initialization independently on each inner and full split; 6000 updates, batch 16, fixed final EMA, mild spatial/intensity augmentation and a half case-uniform, half patient-uniform training law. A finite smoke forward/backward and inner preference Dice >=0.78 precede full fit. No external medical images or neighboring slices are inputs.
