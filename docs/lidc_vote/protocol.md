## Hypothesis

The number of positive votes per pixel measures a different quantity from the area rank of a whole contour. Train jointly for both: four aligned mask heads and four cumulative vote-support targets (at least 1, 2, 3 or 4 positive expert votes) calculated before image augmentation. The cumulative targets represent the union, the intersection and both intermediate supports. Learn these independently of the downstream metric thresholds. Retain the v1 signed-distance auxiliary task. Initialize from the matching v1 inner or full Cartesian checkpoint. Train two views, full field and an 80-pixel central field enlarged to 128, with continuous rotation, reflection, mild zoom and intensity perturbation around the fixed crop center. All inferences use only the original patch; outside the zoom view use the full-field output. Equal-weight full/zoom logits inside the view with a smooth transition at its edge. Fixed 4000 additional updates per view, batch 24, final EMA. Use original v1 splits and final checkpoints. The engineering gate is finite model updates and correct reconstruction of the two view geometries.
