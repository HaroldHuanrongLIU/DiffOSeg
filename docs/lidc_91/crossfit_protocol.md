# Patient-cross-fitted boundary correction

Weak logit blends and synthetic guide perturbations may fail to reproduce real single-slice prediction errors. Train three auxiliary segmentation generators per outer fold on two thirds of the inner-training patients each. The remaining third supplies genuinely out-of-fit masks for each image. Use only the original LIDC labels. Neither the outer patients nor the inner calibration/check patients enter any auxiliary generator fit. Fix per-patient thirds by random seed 93011+outer_fold. Train registered scratch U-Nets for 4500 steps per split with a matched aligned CT, four area ranks, cumulative votes, signed distance and radius/landmark loss. The generators see only their own patients when estimating registration. At prediction, use image-only localization or a fixed central origin, including when generating training examples for the correction. Disclose whether guides come from the smaller generators or the stronger frozen incumbent.

Use these real errors to learn a bounded residual on the preference predictions with local CT and global shape information; evaluate radius calibration and probabilistic mask corrections separately. Fix correction training prior to test. The check half of the original inner validation determines whether there is a plausible route to the unrounded 0.91 target. Do not count training or calibration metrics as outer evidence.
