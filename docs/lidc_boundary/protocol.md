# LIDC rank-conditioned boundary correction, protocol v1

Baseline: E11 e11_lidc_formal_20261104a, summary SHA256 d27211bd93bc8eb2eccd728bd9a04840dfb0e7864ec0d15cd7826c782edeb2a0. Use identical LIDC bytes (6c401c96b0e1737d43fdccfc446cb0c3601473d3b7668ad4a68d22ec0c1ee81b), patient manifest order, stable foreground-area ranks, four folds and unweighted mean of fold means. The baseline and some historical error analyses were already known. No new candidate outer scores have been inspected. This is a fixed-protocol retrospective comparison on the published dataset. Generalization to uncropped CT or unseen institutions requires separate testing.

## Hypothesis and design

The DiffOSeg stages estimate population samples and individual mask preferences separately. A shared image-conditioned model trained on all four ranked contours can correct systematic localization and boundary errors without drawing a new diffusion trajectory. A residual term should retain within-rank variation in the consensus samples. The training patches are already centered around a lesion. Absolute patch coordinates are available to both methods at inference, and are an explicit prior here. Do not use coordinates derived from held-out contours, neighboring slices, patient identity, or post-hoc expert assignment.

Use a residual 2D U-Net with the image and coordinate channels, four rank mask logits and four auxiliary signed-distance outputs. Train with foreground soft Dice, binary cross-entropy and bounded distance loss. Use D4 geometry, mild affine and intensity augmentation. Fixed 4000 updates, batch 24, AdamW, cosine learning rate, final EMA and D4 logit averaging. No external data or pretrained weights for the corrector. Equal supervision across ranks. An image-only corrector is a component ablation, not a replacement for the diffusion experiment.

For Stage II use a convex combination of the signed distances of the four frozen DiffOSeg outputs and the corresponding image-predicted masks (0.60 image model, 0.40 diffusion). For Stage I keep each original prefix of K=10/30/50 test-time diffusion draws; assign samples to quartiles by their own predicted foreground area using stable ties. In each quartile add 0.80 of the original deviation from its mean signed-distance field to 0.60 times the image-model contour field plus 0.40 times that mean. Threshold signed distance at zero. Clip fields at eight pixels. This is a joint estimator for a bank of K samples; process each K independently. Inference consumes images and baseline predictions only. Same diffusion draws and trained checkpoints, plus the auxiliary training and inference budget. Baseline and component-only controls are scored alongside the fusion. Preserve the original GED empty-mask convention.

## Validation and outcome

For each fold reserve a deterministic 20% of its outer training patients for inner validation (seed 7310 + fold); fit the corrector only on the remaining inner training patients. The initial sanity gate is finite outputs and aligned inner mean Dice >= 0.80 for each fold. Do not use the archived baseline checkpoint for inner validation because it trained on those patients. No hyperparameter search on outer labels. After validating the method, refit the same fixed recipe using all outer training patients. The final checkpoint is selected by the fixed update budget. Record seeds, step counts, input and output hashes, environment, patient partitions and code versions. GPU engineering smoke uses synthetic/inner-training cases only. Keep other jobs, baseline outputs and Git checkouts intact.

Success requires all three unweighted four-fold point estimates improving: GED@50 lower than 0.11689859202054222, Dice_soft@50 higher than 0.8950999489811244, and aligned Stage II Dice_mean higher than 0.8802246254707158. Also report K=10/30, expert-wise Dice, fold-level changes and paired patient-cluster bootstrap intervals with folds stratified, preserving within-fold slice weighting (5000 replicates). Confidence intervals qualify the findings; a point-estimate win and statistical evidence are separate. Report failed gates and adverse secondary results. Do not use any outer candidate result to pick another configuration for these folds. Archive bank-derived predictions and validate against the baseline masks, case order and summary before reporting.
