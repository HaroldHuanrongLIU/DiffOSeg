## Hypothesis and fitting

An estimated contour provides a local inward normal. Intensities on both sides of that contour may resolve parts of the boundary that are ambiguous from coarse, independent vote logits. Compute a smoothed positive-inside guide distance D0, its unit gradient, and bilinear CT readings at signed offsets ±1.5, ±3, ±6 native pixels. A small multiscale residual CNN sees the original image, local contrast, patch coordinates, guide distance, normal and relative intensity readings. Predict eight bounded displacements relative to D0: four ranked expert distances and four cumulative vote distances. Supervise each by foreground overlap, binary cross-entropy and a near-boundary signed-distance loss. Drop profile information on 25% of training examples to support the same-weights ablation.

Train on synthetic coarse guides constructed from the corresponding training contours with random rater/vote choice, smooth deformation, shift and dilation/erosion; the target rater contours stay in the CT coordinate frame. At inference the guide is the mean of the two middle vote distance fields predicted from the CT by frozen v3. No patient identity, neighboring slices or held-out labels enter the predictor. Fit an independent inner and full refiner per fold. Fixed 5000 updates, batch 20, AdamW and final EMA. Gate full refit on finite smoke passes and inner refiner-only preference Dice >=0.78. Freeze original source and compare the same sample prefixes K=10,30,50.

## Combination and gate

Retain the previous rater/energy calibrators, unchanged. On the inner calibration library only, choose preference mixture by highest mean aligned Dice among {0, .25, .5, .75, 1}, blending native v3 preference logits with the new boundary logits before fixed v4 joint decoding. For consensus choose the same grid of vote mixtures and blend of raw versus joint cumulative supports {0,.5,1} using actual surrogate sample banks. Optimize the smaller of relative GED@50 and soft Dice@50 improvements against v4 full on library patients, with fixed enumeration tie order. Use the original diffusion distance ordering and v4's saved frequency readout. When generating library proposals exclude templates from the same patient. Use the fixed v2 inner image-only proposal for development; a real E11 checkpoint trained on these patients would leak validation supervision. Check parameters on the separate selection half; acknowledge its historical use for v3 and earlier research. Promote only if fold-equal held-check means improve in all three directions against v4 full. Keep failed inner gates. Lock all fold parameters before any new outer prediction, seal all outer banks before scoring, and report all adverse folds/secondary outcomes.

Matched frozen-weight controls: replace lateral profile readings by the center reading at inference, and remove the refiner entirely while retaining the newly chosen combination rule. Report both controls as component interventions. Four-fold success requires lower GED@50, higher Dice_soft@50 and higher aligned Dice_mean than v4 full. Summarize paired differences using 5000 within-fold patient-cluster resamples and equal fold averages, seed 212903. Retain comparison with standalone best v4 ablations regardless of their direction. No claim of external generalization.
